Common Questions

Visa FAQs

How long does visa processing take?

Most tourist and business visas process in 3–7 working days once documents are complete. Student and work visas can take longer depending on the embassy.

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No agency can guarantee approval — that decision sits with the embassy. What we guarantee is a complete, error-checked application to give you the best possible chance.

Can I apply if I'm travelling in under two weeks?

Yes — reach out and we'll flag whether expedited processing is available for your destination and let you know realistic timelines before you pay anything.

What if my visa is rejected?

We review the embassy's reasoning with you at no extra cost and help prepare a stronger re-application where one is possible.

Do I need to submit original documents or copies?

It depends on the embassy — some accept scanned copies while others require originals or notarized translations. Your checklist will spell out exactly what's needed for your destination.

Can you help with visas for group or family travel?

Yes — we regularly manage group and family applications together, keeping every traveler's documents organized and appointments scheduled on the same timeline.
